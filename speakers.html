MediaForge Summit 2026

Our Speakers

180+ world-class voices from media, technology, journalism, and policy — shaping the conversations that define the future of global communications.

MediaForge Summit 2026 brings together an exceptional roster of speakers drawn from the foremost organisations in global media and technology. Our curation process prioritises intellectual rigour, diversity of perspective, and the direct relevance of each speaker's expertise to the challenges facing the industry today.

Below is a selection of confirmed speakers. The full programme will be published in January 2026. Submit a speaker proposal if you would like to be considered.

Dr. Amara Osei

CEO, NexStream Media  ·  Ghana
Keynote

"AI and the Future of African Journalism"

Dr. Amara Osei has led NexStream Media from a regional broadcaster to one of Africa's fastest-growing digital media platforms. A leading voice on AI-driven content localisation and pan-African media development, she advises the African Union on digital communications policy and has been recognised by the World Economic Forum as a Young Global Leader.

Marcus Lindqvist

CTO, EuroMediaGroup  ·  Sweden
Keynote

"Scalable Streaming Architectures in the 5G Era"

Marcus Lindqvist oversees the technology strategy for EuroMediaGroup's portfolio of 40+ broadcast and digital channels across 12 European countries. A pioneer in cloud-native media production, he previously led engineering teams at two of the world's largest streaming platforms and holds patents in adaptive bitrate streaming.

Yuki Tanaka

Editor-in-Chief, Pacific Digital Press  ·  Japan
Panel

"Audience Trust in the Age of Synthetic Media"

Yuki Tanaka has spent fifteen years investigating the relationship between digital technology and public trust in journalism. Under her editorship, Pacific Digital Press received the International Press Freedom Award for its pioneering coverage of deepfake detection in news media. She serves on the UNESCO Advisory Panel on Digital Information Integrity.

Fatima Al-Rasheed

Director of Digital Strategy, Al-Mashriq Broadcasting  ·  UAE
Panel

"Monetising Arabic-Language Content Globally"

Fatima Al-Rasheed is recognised as one of the MENA region's foremost digital media strategists. Her work at Al-Mashriq Broadcasting has tripled Arabic-language streaming revenue through targeted content partnerships and subscription innovation. She was named Arab Media Leader of the Year by the Dubai Press Club in 2024.

Dr. Carlos Vega

Professor of Media Innovation, University of Buenos Aires  ·  Argentina
Panel

"Community Journalism and Hyperlocal Platforms"

Dr. Carlos Vega is a leading academic researcher in community-driven media models across Latin America. He has consulted for UNESCO on digital literacy programmes and is the author of three books on the future of local journalism in the platform era. His research on hyperlocal news sustainability has been adopted by governments in six countries.

Eleanor Chambers

Co-Founder, VoxBridge AI  ·  United Kingdom
Workshop

"Building Ethical AI Tools for Newsrooms"

Eleanor Chambers co-founded VoxBridge AI after a decade as a technology editor at national newspapers. Her work focuses on developing transparent, explainable AI systems that support — rather than replace — editorial judgement in modern newsrooms. VoxBridge AI's tools are now used by over 300 news organisations worldwide.
