Your first visit

How we work with you.

And what to expect on your first visit.

Book an Appointment
  1. 1

    Consultation & Exam

    We'll talk with you about your condition and concerns, your medical history, and your goals. And we'll do an assessment to get a good picture of your overall health.

  2. 2

    Diagnostic Testing

    We may require further testing to determine the right course of care for you. Most tests, such as x-rays and posture scans, can be performed right in our office.

  3. 3

    Report of Findings

    We recommend a treatment plan tailored specifically for you. Plans are designed to help you achieve optimal short- and long-term results as quickly as possible—and with your budget in mind, to keep things affordable.
