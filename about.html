About MediaForge Summit

Where Technology Meets Media. Where Ideas Become Industry.

Established 2014 — Now in its 11th Edition

Our Mission

MediaForge Summit exists to accelerate the convergence of media and technology by convening the world’s most influential minds in journalism, broadcasting, digital content, and emerging communications technology. Through rigorous dialogue, curated programming, and high-impact networking, we cultivate the partnerships and insights that define the next era of global media.

The Summit

MediaForge Summit is a premier international conference dedicated to the intersection of technology, media, and communications. Each year, we bring together executives, innovators, policymakers, and creatives from across the globe to address the most pressing challenges and opportunities facing the media industry.

Our programming spans three full days of keynote addresses, expert panels, hands-on workshops, and curated networking sessions. Attendees gain unparalleled access to thought leaders shaping the future of digital journalism, streaming, AI-driven content creation, and audience engagement strategies.

Whether you are a media executive seeking strategic clarity, a technologist building the platforms of tomorrow, or an emerging professional forging your path, MediaForge Summit provides the environment, the connections, and the knowledge to propel your work forward.

What We Stand For

Three principles guide every decision we make and every programme we design.

Innovation

We champion bold ideas and emerging technologies. Our programme intentionally surfaces disruptive thinking, ensuring that attendees leave equipped to lead — not merely adapt to — industry change.

Influence

Media shapes society. We take that responsibility seriously by platforming voices that drive meaningful, ethical, and lasting impact — across borders, industries, and communities.

Integrity

Every speaker, sponsor, and session is curated to the highest editorial standard. We maintain full independence in our programming to ensure our content serves our community — not commercial agendas.

Conference Highlights

Three days of world-class programming designed for media professionals at every stage.

Keynote Addresses

Headline sessions from global CEOs, award-winning journalists, and government ministers on the forces reshaping media and technology.

Workshops & Masterclasses

Intensive, practitioner-led sessions covering AI in newsrooms, monetisation strategies, audience analytics, and next-generation storytelling formats.

Executive Networking

Structured roundtables, hosted dinners, and curated one-to-one meetings facilitating high-value connections between senior industry professionals.

Technology Exhibition

A dedicated exhibition floor showcasing the latest platforms, tools, and solutions from leading technology providers and ambitious media start-ups.
