Why Attend MediaForge Summit?

Three days of world-class content, unrivalled networking, and actionable insights that will transform how you lead in an industry defined by constant change.

World-Class Keynotes

Hear from the most influential CEOs, editors, technologists, and policymakers driving the global media agenda — with perspectives spanning six continents.

Executive Networking

Connect with 4,200+ senior professionals through structured roundtables, hosted dinners, and curated one-to-one meetings designed for meaningful outcomes.

Practical Masterclasses

Gain skills you can apply immediately through practitioner-led workshops on AI, content monetisation, audience analytics, and responsible technology.

Six Tracks. One Transformative Summit.

Every session, workshop, and keynote is organised around six defining themes that reflect the most pressing forces reshaping media and technology today.

12 sessions

Streaming & Distribution

The Venue

MediaForge Summit 2026 takes place at the ExCeL Centre — London's premier international events venue, located in the Royal Docks in east London. With over 100,000 sqm of flexible event space, ExCeL Centre provides the ideal setting for a conference of our scale and ambition.
