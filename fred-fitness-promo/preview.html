Questions

Do you have humans there?

Yes! We have a lovely team that provides ongoing support. We also offer human-led classes, such as Mat Pilates, which are complimentary to our members.

What is AI-powered fitness?

Your fitness assessment data, preferences, chat input, and goal selections are processed to generate a tailored training plan that is based on exercise science. All equipment adapts to your height, strength, range of motion, and your plan – completely removing the guesswork from your workout.

I’m a beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced athlete, the equipment adapts to your preferences and goals, and our team is there to help you get started.

Is there a day pass?

Yes! Your first day pass is free ($40 thereafter). If you’re just visiting, we also offer week passes.
